How to play

Talk in your Starmaker room. Use this site only for the board.

The idea

Two teams - Red and Blue - compete to find their own words first. Each team has a captain who can see the secret key.

Colours

Red team word Blue team word Bystander (no team) Assassin (lose instantly)

Turn flow

  1. The captain gives a one-word clue and a number by voice.
  2. Any player may submit a guess on the board.
  3. The host reveals or rejects the guess.
  4. A correct team word continues the turn. A wrong guess or bystander passes the turn.
  5. Finding the assassin ends the round - the other team wins.
  6. The first team to find all of their words wins the round.

One session, many rounds

The same invite code works for the whole Starmaker session. Teams, captain and board are set fresh for every round. Only the host can end the session.
